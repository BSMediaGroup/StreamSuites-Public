Access by design

Clear paths through every public surface.

StreamSuites aims to keep its public experience readable, operable, and understandable across input methods, screen sizes, and motion preferences.

Active practice

Accessibility is an ongoing product responsibility. This page describes current design intentions and known limits; it is not a certification or formal conformance claim.

Report a barrier

Current principles

Practical support, built into the interface.

The public site uses native controls and clear content structure wherever possible. These practices are reviewed as individual surfaces change, while coverage across every StreamSuites product remains a work in progress.

01 / Keyboard

Keyboard and focus

Navigation, links, forms, expandable roadmap items, and primary actions are intended to remain reachable without a pointer. Visible focus rings identify the current control.

Tab Enter Esc
02 / Readability

Contrast and typography

Body copy uses Geist Sans at deliberate reading widths, Tektur is reserved for display hierarchy, and technical status text uses IBM Plex Mono. Muted text and controls are checked against the dark surfaces they occupy.

03 / Motion

Reduced motion

Decorative reveals and progress transitions respond to the operating-system reduced-motion preference. Information remains present when animation is removed.

04 / Reflow

Responsive layouts

Navigation, content grids, legal reading layouts, and controls reflow for narrow viewports without requiring horizontal page scrolling.

05 / Semantics

Meaningful structure

Landmarks, ordered headings, labelled controls, status announcements, and native disclosure elements provide a stronger foundation for assistive technologies.

Known limits

Honest about what is still being verified.

  • StreamSuites has not published an independent accessibility audit or certification.
  • Coverage can vary between the public site, Browser Studio, native StudioApp, OBS integration, and third-party provider interfaces.
  • Discord, payment, status, authentication, media, and other embedded or external services retain their own accessibility behavior.
  • The assistive-technology and physical-device test matrix is not yet comprehensive.
Feedback

If something blocks access, describe the page, control, device, browser, and assistive technology involved through the support hub or email support@streamsuites.app.

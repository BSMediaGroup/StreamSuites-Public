What support means

Backing the project, without invented promises.

Contributions support the work required to build, operate, document, package, and test StreamSuites. Allocation varies with current project needs, and no individual donation is assigned to a claimed unit of infrastructure or feature delivery.

Build + operate

Product and platform work

Support can contribute to the ongoing engineering and operation behind the Runtime/Auth authority, public and creator-facing clients, Browser Studio, native StudioApp, OBS integration, and their supporting infrastructure.
